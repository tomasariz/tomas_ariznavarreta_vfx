About me

Principal VFX Artist specializing in video games, with experience in large-scale productions such as Asphalt, Disney Speedstorm and Invizimals. I combine a strong artistic vision with technical expertise and experience in VFX team leadership and coordination, contributing to visual effects that enhance player experience and strengthen gameplay communication.

My expertise spans the creation and implementation of VFX for vehicles, characters, special abilities, environments, and atmospheric systems, as well as the development of visual solutions for complex gameplay mechanics. I have worked closely with Engine and Programming teams on the development of tools and shaders, helping adapt engine capabilities to the project’s artistic and functional requirements.

I have taken on VFX leadership responsibilities, organizing workflows, assigning tasks, coordinating priorities, and guiding VFX development in collaboration with the team. I work cross-functionally with Art Direction, Art, Animation, Design, Programming, Engine, and Production to ensure visual consistency, technical quality, and seamless gameplay integration, while aligning VFX with the project’s overall artistic vision.

I’m available for freelance projects and studio positions.
